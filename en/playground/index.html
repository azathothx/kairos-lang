Kairos Playground

Evaluate Kairos expressions in the browser (the reference implementation runs as is — nothing is sent to any server). The language is at the RC (release candidate) stage — see the language spec and the descriptor reference; the workbooks and sample questions are in Japanese.

Results appear here.

The evaluation range is the half-open interval [from, to) — from is included, to is excluded. In the output, # ⚠ marks annotations of out-of-range provenance and # coverage summary reports the data runway (spec §4.10). "Pin to URL" produces a shareable link carrying the expression and range. Messages produced by the evaluator itself — syntax errors and annotation texts — appear in Japanese, the implementation's canonical output language; the English spec defines their exact semantics.
